Privacy Policy

How Gilbert Handymen Services handles information you share with us.

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Gilbert Handymen Services (“we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use gilberthandymen.com or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, phone, email, city, project description)
  • Basic technical data from website hosting/analytics if enabled (IP, browser type, pages viewed)

How we use information

  • To respond to quote requests and schedule work
  • To communicate about jobs you request
  • To improve our website and service (aggregated/anonymous where possible)

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share details with insurers or subcontractors only as needed to perform work you requested, or when required by law.
