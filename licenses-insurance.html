Licenses & Insurance

Insured handyman service for Gilbert and the East Valley.

Not a Licensed Contractor.

Gilbert Handymen Services provides casual and minor handyman work in Gilbert, AZ and nearby East Valley communities. Under Arizona law, certain casual/minor projects under $1,000 that do not require a building permit may be performed without an Arizona ROC contractor license. We are not a licensed contractor.

We do not perform panel upgrades, new circuit wiring, unrestricted plumbing installations, or other permit/licensed-trade work. If your project needs a licensed electrician, plumber, or general contractor, we will say so up front and help you find the right next step.

Insurance

We maintain active general liability insurance for the handyman services we offer. A certificate of insurance is available on request for homeowners, HOAs, and property managers.

What we do

  • Fixture swaps and small electrical handyman tasks within scope
  • Faucet and toilet repairs
  • Drywall patches
  • Painting and accent walls
  • Trim and carpentry fixes
  • Ceiling fans and similar minor home repairs
