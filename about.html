About Gilbert Handymen Services

Local handyman help for Gilbert and the East Valley — founded in 2013, focused on clear estimates and careful work.

Gilbert Handymen Services has been helping East Valley homeowners with practical repairs since 2013. We’re a service-area business based in Gilbert, AZ (ZIP 85297) — not a storefront walk-in shop.

Our approach is simple: listen to what you need, confirm the job is within handyman scope, give you a clear price, show up on time, and leave the work looking finished. When something needs a licensed electrician, plumber, or general contractor, we say so up front instead of forcing a bad fit.

What we stand for

  • Transparent pricing and communication
  • Insured service for the work we take on
  • Respect for your home — prep, protection, and cleanup
  • Honest scope: handyman repairs, not licensed-trade overselling
