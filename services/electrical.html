Electrical Handyman Help in Gilbert, AZ

Ceiling fans, fixtures, switches, and outlets — carefully done within handyman scope.

Need a ceiling fan hung, a light fixture swapped, or a simple outlet/switch replacement done carefully? Gilbert Handymen Services handles common electrical handyman jobs in Gilbert and the East Valley. We are fully insured, give you a clear estimate up front, and stay in touch until the job is done.

If your project needs a licensed electrician — panel upgrades, new circuits, rewiring, or permit work — we will tell you honestly and help you get the right specialist instead of forcing a bad fit.

What we help with

  • Ceiling fan installation and replacement (existing box/support confirmed safe)
  • Light fixture, switch, and outlet replacements on existing circuits
  • Smoke/CO detector installs where wiring is already present
  • Troubleshooting simple fixture issues and basic connectivity
  • Tidying and securing after fixture work so the finish looks clean

What we do not advertise or take on

  • Electrical panel upgrades or service upgrades
  • New wiring runs or adding new circuits
  • Full electrical inspections marketed as code-compliance contracting
  • Permit-required electrical construction

Why homeowners call us

Transparent pricing, on-time arrival, careful cleanup, and clear communication. Founded in 2013, we focus on practical East Valley home repairs — not overselling licensed trade work.

FAQ

Are you insured?

Yes. Gilbert Handymen Services carries general liability insurance for the handyman services we perform.

Can you upgrade my electrical panel?

No. Panel upgrades and new circuit work belong with a licensed electrician. We can help with fixture-level handyman tasks and point you to licensed help when needed.

Do you pull electrical permits?

We do not market permit-required electrical contracting. If a job needs a permit or licensed electrician, we say so before work starts.
