Bee Removal & Repair in Gilbert, AZ

Live bee removal and relocation by a beekeeper who keeps bees — then the handyman repairs to put your walls and ceilings back together.

Finding a hive in a wall, eaves, or attic is stressful. Gilbert Handymen Services handles both sides of the job: carefully removing and relocating the bees when possible, and repairing the drywall, stucco access points, and finishes afterward so your home is sealed and tidy again.

We keep bees ourselves. That shapes how we work — the goal is a safe removal for your household and a chance for the colony to survive when relocation is realistic, not a spray-and-walk-away approach.

What we help with

  • Assessment of accessible hives in walls, soffits, eaves, sheds, and similar spots
  • Live removal and relocation when conditions allow
  • Coordination of the removal plan with clear communication on timing and access
  • Opening and closing chase walls or ceilings as needed to reach the nest
  • Drywall, texture, paint touch-up, and sealing after the bees are out
  • Cleanup of comb, honey residue, and debris from the work area when part of the agreed scope

What this service is — and is not

  • Is: Beekeeper-led removal/relocation plus handyman repair of the structure afterward
  • Is not: A claim that we are a licensed pest-control company for every insect or chemical treatment program
  • Aggressive Africanized colonies, extreme heights, or jobs that need specialized pest licensing or equipment may be referred or scoped differently — we will tell you honestly after looking at the situation

Why combine removal and repair

Removing bees without fixing the opening leaves you with holes, weak spots, and a mess. Doing the repair without proper removal risks trapping bees in the wall. We treat it as one project: get the colony out safely, then restore the area so it looks and works like a finished home again.

FAQ

Do you kill the bees?

Whenever practical we prioritize live removal and relocation. We keep bees and prefer giving colonies a chance when it can be done safely for your family and for us.

Will you repair the wall after?

Yes — that is a core part of this service. Drywall, access openings, and finish work after removal are included in the scope we quote up front.

Are you insured?

Yes. Gilbert Handymen Services carries general liability insurance for the services we perform. Ask us for a certificate of insurance if your HOA or property manager needs one.
